Breaking Bad

Breaking Bad is an American neo-Western crime drama television
series created and produced by Vince Gilligan. A failing husband
turns to a life of crime and drug dealing to make money after figuring
out he has lung cancer and has to treat it. But the greed for money
leads him down a dark path.

Spider-Man: The Animated Series

Spider-Man: The Animated Series is an American animated television
series based on the Marvel Comics character Spider-Man. The show follows
a version of peter parker based in the 60s living with his aunt after the
death of his parents and his uncle Ben. He must balance his dual life as a student
and a superhero while facing various villains and challenges.
